My personal portfolio site — a single page covering my background, experience, and ways to connect, plus a small markets-data API that feeds the page.

I'm based in Houston and speak Chinese and English. I have a Master of Finance from Tulane University and work in commercial real estate, focusing on financial modeling, valuation, and investment research.

I'm also researching crypto and quantitative modeling, and exploring how AI can make financial research more useful. I'm open to opportunities in private equity and investment banking.

## What's on the site

- **About** — background and focus areas
- **Experience** — work and project history
- **Connect** — contact links
- **Markets API** — `app/api/markets/route.ts` serves market data to the page

## Tech stack

- Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS, `lucide-react` icons
